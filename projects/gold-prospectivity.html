Gold Mineralisation Prospectivity Mapping

Shamva District, Mashonaland Central, Zimbabwe

Project Overview

This project focuses on mapping gold mineralisation prospectivity in Shamva District, Zimbabwe, using Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing and spatial analysis techniques.

The objective was to identify areas with different levels of potential for gold mineralisation and provide spatial information that can support mineral exploration and decision-making.

Gold Prospectivity Map

Methodology

The gold prospectivity analysis integrated multiple spatial datasets representing geological, structural, hydrothermal and environmental characteristics associated with gold mineralisation.

  • Existing gold occurrence data
  • Lithology
  • Hydrothermal alteration
  • Lineament density
  • Drainage density
  • Land Use/Land Cover
  • Land Surface Temperature

The thematic layers were standardised and combined using a weighted overlay approach to produce the final Gold Mineralisation Prospectivity map.

Prospectivity Classes

  • Very Low: 14%
  • Low: 32%
  • Moderate: 27%
  • High: 16%
  • Very High: 11%

Validation

The resulting prospectivity map was evaluated using seven known gold occurrence locations. Most of the known occurrences were located within areas classified as High or Very High prospectivity.
