Land Use and Land Cover Mapping

Satellite Remote Sensing and Machine Learning

Project Overview

This project involved the development of a Land Use and Land Cover (LULC) classification map using satellite remote sensing and machine learning techniques.

The objective was to identify and map major land-cover classes within the study area and generate spatial information that can support environmental monitoring, land management and geospatial decision-making.

Methodology

Sentinel-2 multispectral satellite imagery was acquired and prepared for land-cover classification. The imagery was processed to support supervised classification of the study area.

Training samples were developed for the identified land-cover classes. A machine-learning classification workflow was then implemented in Google Earth Engine to produce the LULC classification.

The classified imagery was subsequently subjected to accuracy assessment to evaluate the performance of the classification. The final result was exported and prepared for GIS visualisation and Web GIS integration.

Land-Cover Classes

Water Bodies

Rivers, dams and other surface-water features.

Built-up Areas

Buildings, settlements and developed surfaces.

Croplands

Agricultural land used for crop production.

Vegetation

Vegetated areas classified separately from wooded grassland.

Wooded Grassland

Areas characterised by grassland with scattered woody vegetation.

Bare Land

Exposed soil and other surfaces with limited vegetation cover.

Applications

The resulting LULC dataset can support land management, environmental monitoring, agricultural assessment, settlement analysis and spatial decision-making.

The LULC layer can also be integrated with other geospatial datasets within a Web GIS environment to support interactive spatial visualisation and analysis.
