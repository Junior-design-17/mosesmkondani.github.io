Groundwater Potential Zone Modelling

Integration of GIS, remote sensing and geophysical information to model groundwater potential zones in Mapinga, Mashonaland West, Zimbabwe.

← Back to Portfolio
01

Project Overview

Groundwater potential zone modelling was undertaken in Mapinga, Mashonaland West, Zimbabwe, using an integrated GIS and remote sensing approach.

The project involved the preparation and integration of multiple thematic datasets representing terrain, drainage, surface characteristics, geological structures and land-surface conditions.

The resulting thematic information was combined through a weighted overlay approach to produce a groundwater potential zone map.

Study Area

Mapinga, Mashonaland West, Zimbabwe
