ABOUT

Spatial thinking.
Real-world solutions.

I am a Geospatial Scientist focused on using GIS, Remote Sensing and Earth Observation to understand spatial patterns and solve real-world environmental and resource management problems.

My work combines spatial analysis, satellite imagery, UAV mapping, GNSS surveying, geophysical methods and Web GIS technologies to transform geospatial data into useful information for decision-making.

My project experience includes groundwater potential modelling, mineral prospectivity mapping, land-use and land-cover classification, UAV topographic surveys and interactive Web GIS applications.

I am particularly interested in the application of geospatial technologies to natural resources, environmental monitoring, mineral exploration and Earth observation.
