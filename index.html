EARTH OBSERVATION • LAND COVER MAPPING

Land Use & Land Cover Classification

Remote Sensing and GIS Analysis

A satellite-based Land Use and Land Cover (LULC) mapping project using Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery and Google Earth Engine to classify and analyse the spatial distribution of major land cover classes.

Land Use and Land Cover Classification Map

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Mapping the Landscape

Land Use and Land Cover mapping provides important spatial information for understanding how landscapes are distributed and used. This project applies Earth observation data and GIS techniques to produce a thematic LULC map showing the distribution of different land-cover classes.

The resulting map can support environmental monitoring, land management, agricultural planning, natural-resource assessment and spatial decision-making.

DATA & TECHNOLOGY

Data and Tools

Satellite Data

Sentinel-2 multispectral Earth observation imagery was used as the primary satellite data source.

Processing Platform

Google Earth Engine was used for satellite image processing, classification and accuracy assessment.

GIS

GIS techniques were used for spatial preparation, visualisation, analysis and map production.

METHODOLOGY

LULC Mapping Workflow

STEP 01

Satellite Data Preparation

Sentinel-2 imagery was prepared for analysis and restricted to the study area.

STEP 02

Image Processing

Relevant spectral information was prepared for use in the supervised classification workflow.

STEP 03

Training Data

Representative training samples were prepared for the identified land-cover classes.

STEP 04

Classification

A supervised machine-learning classification workflow was applied to generate the LULC classes.

STEP 05

Accuracy Assessment

Classification performance was evaluated using overall accuracy and the Kappa coefficient.

STEP 06

Map Production

The classified output was prepared and visualised as a final LULC map for interpretation and presentation.

CLASSIFICATION

Land Cover Classes

The classification identified six major land-cover classes within the study area.

Water Bodies Built-up Areas Croplands Vegetation Wooded Grassland Bare Land
