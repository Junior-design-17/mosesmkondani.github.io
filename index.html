ABOUT ME

Geospatial Science Meets Real-World Problems.

I am a Geospatial Scientist with a background in Geographic Information Science and Earth Observation, with practical experience in GIS, remote sensing, spatial modelling, UAV surveying and Web GIS.

My work focuses on transforming spatial and Earth observation data into useful information for environmental monitoring, mineral exploration, natural-resource assessment and spatial decision-making.

I have worked with satellite imagery, digital elevation models, GNSS observations, drone imagery and spatial databases to develop analytical workflows and geospatial applications.
