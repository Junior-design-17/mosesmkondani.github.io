About Me

I am a Geospatial Scientist specialising in Geographic Information Systems, Remote Sensing, Earth Observation, Web GIS, UAV mapping and GNSS surveying.

My work focuses on transforming spatial data into practical information that supports environmental management, mineral exploration, groundwater assessment, land-use planning and spatial decision-making.

I have experience working with GIS databases, satellite imagery, digital elevation models, spatial analysis, geospatial modelling, field surveying and interactive Web GIS applications.

FEATURED PROJECT

Web GIS Gold Claim Pegging Decision-Support System

An interactive Web GIS decision-support system developed for visualising gold mineralisation prospectivity and supporting spatial decision-making for gold claim pegging in Shamva District, Zimbabwe.

The system integrates geospatial datasets, prospectivity information, farm boundaries and interactive mapping functionality.
